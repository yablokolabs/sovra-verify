HELVETIA PRAEZISION GMBH - ACCOUNTS PAYABLE POLICY

Document owner: Finance Operations
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Applies to: all supplier invoices, including invoices received from Nordwind
Logistik AG under the master supply agreement.

Policy 4.1 Standard settlement period
All supplier invoices are settled within 60 days of the invoice date. Finance
Operations processes the payment run on the last working day of each month.

Policy 4.4 Invoice approval
An invoice is released for payment only after three-way matching against the
purchase order and the goods receipt record has been completed.

Policy 6.2 Currency
Supplier invoices are recorded in Swiss francs (CHF). Foreign-currency invoices
are converted at the Swiss National Bank reference rate on the invoice date.
