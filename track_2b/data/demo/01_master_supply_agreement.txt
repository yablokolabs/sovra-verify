MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

Parties: Nordwind Logistik AG (Supplier) and Helvetia Praezision GmbH (Buyer)
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Term: until 2028-12-31

Clause 4.1 Scope
This agreement governs all road-freight logistics services supplied by Nordwind
Logistik AG to Helvetia Praezision GmbH under purchase orders referencing this
agreement.

Clause 7.2 Payment terms
The Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.
Payments shall be made in CHF by bank transfer to the account stated on the
invoice.

Clause 7.5 Expedited freight exception
Invoices issued under the expedited freight programme described in Schedule C
are payable within 10 days of the invoice date. Clause 7.2 does not apply to
those invoices.

Clause 9.3 Currency
All amounts are stated in Swiss francs (CHF). Amounts invoiced in another
currency are converted at the Swiss National Bank reference rate on the invoice
date.

Clause 11.2 Disputed invoices
The Buyer may withhold payment of a disputed amount provided it notifies the
Supplier in writing within 10 days of receipt of the invoice.
